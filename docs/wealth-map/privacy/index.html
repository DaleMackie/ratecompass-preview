THE WEALTH MAP / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Publisher: Freewind Life Online Ltd.

Privacy and support contact: thewealthmap@gmail.com

Your information stays under your control

The Wealth Map is a financial education and planning app. This version works with a local profile and does not require an online account, email address, or password. The app does not send your profile or planning entries to the developer. It does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking, or automatic bank connections.

What is saved on your device

Depending on the features you use, the app saves your name or nickname, profile answers (including age range, gender, province, household details, dependants, experience, and life stage), lesson and quiz completion, action progress, deferred-action dates, earned coins, and unlocked tools.

It also saves information you choose to enter in planning worksheets, such as income, expenses, assets, debts, credit score, savings commitments, emergency-fund goals, birth date for the TFSA worksheet, contribution estimates, investing preferences, estate-planning research, notes, and budget history. You do not need to enter bank credentials, account numbers, a Social Insurance Number, or government-account passwords. Do not put these in free-text fields.

How the app uses this information

Your entries are used on your device to personalize educational next steps, calculate estimates, show progress, and remember your choices. Your information is not sold, used for advertising, or shared by the app with the developer or other companies.

Storage, backups, and security

The app stores this information in its local app preferences. It does not provide its own cloud synchronization, remote account, or recovery service. Apple device backups, device transfers, or restoration may include app data depending on your settings. Those services are controlled by Apple and your device settings.

Protect your device with a passcode and keep it secure. Anyone with access to your unlocked device may be able to read the app. This version does not provide a separate app password or promise additional encryption beyond protections provided by the operating system. Loss of the device or deletion of the app may result in loss of your entries.

Editing and deleting information

Saved entries remain in this installation until you change or clear them. There is no developer-operated server copy or automatic retention schedule for these local entries.

You can edit profile answers and worksheet entries in the app. Profile → Reset Progress clears learning and action progress while keeping your profile and planning entries. Profile → Start Over clears saved profile and planning entries, progress, and the advanced-interest preference from this installation, then returns you to setup. The app may retain empty/default preference values needed to function.

Start Over does not delete previously created device backups, screenshots, or information you have separately shared. Manage backups through Apple’s device and account settings. The developer cannot retrieve or remotely erase entries that have remained on your device.

External websites and support

Some educational links open external websites. The app does not attach your worksheet entries to those links. Visiting a website may share connection information, such as your IP address, with that website, and its own privacy policy applies.

If you email thewealthmap@gmail.com, Freewind Life Online Ltd. receives the email address and information you choose to include. Support messages are handled through Gmail and used to respond to requests, resolve problems, and meet applicable obligations. They are kept only as long as needed for those purposes. You can request deletion of support correspondence by email, subject to any applicable retention obligations. Do not include sensitive financial details or passwords.

Our support website

The support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It displays app information and these disclosures without asking for an account, worksheet entries, or payment details. It includes no advertising, analytics scripts, mailing-list forms, or embedded third-party fonts. GitHub may process connection and technical information, such as an IP address, to serve and protect the website. GitHub and Gmail have their own privacy practices; website visits and support messages are distinct from the app’s local planning entries.

Advanced Wealth Map interest

The optional interest button currently saves only a preference on this device. It does not send information to the developer, join a waiting list, subscribe you to emails, or commit you to a purchase. You can withdraw interest on the completion page or clear it with Start Over.

Changes and contact

These statements describe the current local-only version and the support channels described above. We will update this policy before introducing online accounts, analytics, mailing lists, or other new data transfers, and provide appropriate choices. For privacy questions or support requests, contact Freewind Life Online Ltd. at thewealthmap@gmail.com.
