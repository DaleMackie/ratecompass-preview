Useful things to know

Do I need an account or password?

No. This version uses a local profile. On first use, choose Set Up My Profile. Once setup is complete, returning users go directly to their dashboard.

Where are my entries saved?

Planning entries and learning progress are saved in the app on your device. The app does not provide cloud syncing or a remote recovery service. Apple backups and device transfers may include app data depending on your settings.

How do I reset progress or clear my information?

In Profile, Reset Progress clears learning and action progress while keeping your profile and planning entries. Start Over clears saved local profile and planning entries, progress, and advanced-interest preferences, then returns you to setup. This does not erase older backups or information you have shared separately.

Can you recover entries from a lost device?

We cannot retrieve data that remained on your device. Whether a backup can restore it depends on your Apple settings and available backups.

Does the advanced-interest button join a mailing list?

No. It currently saves an interest preference only on the device. It does not send us your information or subscribe you to emails.

Can I ask for financial advice by email?

This address provides app support. The Wealth Map supplies general financial education, not individualized investment, tax, legal, credit, or insurance advice. For those decisions, consult an appropriately qualified professional.
