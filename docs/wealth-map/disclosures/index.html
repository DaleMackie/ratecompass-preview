Education and planning support

The Wealth Map provides general financial education and tools to help you organize your own planning. It does not provide individualized investment, tax, legal, credit, or insurance advice, and using it does not establish a professional advisory relationship. Profile-based lesson suggestions are educational guidance, not a determination that a product or strategy is suitable for you.

Estimates are not guarantees

Calculators and projections depend on the figures and assumptions entered. Actual interest, fees, taxes, inflation, market performance, account terms, and payment schedules can differ. Illustrations may omit factors relevant to your circumstances. Confirm amounts with your financial institution and current official records before acting. Investing involves risk, including loss of principal; growth, financial freedom, and other outcomes are not guaranteed.

Canadian rules and eligibility

Content focuses on Canada. Registered-account rules, contribution room, government benefits, tax treatment, and estate requirements can change and depend on your circumstances and province or territory. A contribution-room worksheet is an estimate, not an official CRA record. Confirm eligibility, limits, and transactions using current official sources and your own records. Seek qualified professional advice for decisions that require it.

Progress reflects learning

Finishing levels, earning coins, or reaching a progress score recognizes activity in this app. It is not a professional qualification, financial-health assessment, verified comparison with other Canadians, or proof that a financial plan is complete. Coins are in-app progress rewards with no cash value. Completing Level 10 is a learning milestone; continue to review your plan as life changes.

No financial transactions

This version does not connect to bank accounts, move money, execute trades, file taxes, create legally valid estate documents, or enroll you in government services. You remain responsible for steps you choose to take outside the app. External links are provided for reference; the app is not a government service or a financial institution.
